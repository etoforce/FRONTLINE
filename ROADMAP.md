# FRONTLINE Roadmap

The project is in pre-alpha. The latest verified development milestone is **0.46**, which covers directional armor, infantry suppression, vehicle subsystem damage, and paid repairs. The [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) tracks verified development changes under **Unreleased** until they are part of a confirmed release.

## Playable foundation

- **Skirmish and Sandbox** offer a full match against the AI and a free-play mode across three maps.
- **Territory and logistics** cover infantry capture, credit income, supply posts, build coverage, army capacity, production, and field repairs.
- **Combined-arms command** includes autonomous army orders, direct control, artillery, recon, fog of war, and experience from kills.
- **English and Russian** are available from the main menu.

## Verified in development

| Area | Progress |
| --- | --- |
| Routes and formations | Implemented army rally points, alternate crossings, wide fords on River Front and Far Frontier, and lead-vehicle replacement. |
| Fights for crossings | Verified holding before a visible defensive turret, using artillery or flanking infantry where eligible, and resuming the advance when the threat is cleared. |
| Terrain and damage | Added a tactical ridge on River Front, visible artillery impacts, and three stages of structural damage through collapse. |
| Combat and repairs | Verified directional armor, temporary infantry suppression, vehicle subsystem damage, and paid repairs. |
| Interface and presentation | Added a unit information panel, minimap control-group markers, and Cinematic View with animated mist in Compatibility and Forward+. |

## Development priorities

- Profile performance and improve stability in large and long-running battles.
- Refine navigation and army coordination at chokepoints and around construction.
- Run repeatable balance matches across all three maps to evaluate early rushes, territorial defense, armor play, and sieges.
- Prototype a distinct second faction with its own units and viable counters.
- Improve combat readability and animation. Tutorials, difficulty options, and saving and loading remain planned.
