# FRONTLINE Roadmap

## Playable in the development build

| Feature | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Skirmish | Playable prototype with base construction, territory capture, and an AI opponent. |
| Cinematic view | Implemented and tested. The camera can take a lower angle, the interface and tactical markers are hidden, and the battle continues. Ground mist was verified in Compatibility and Forward+. |

## Development priorities

- Improve performance during large battles, unit navigation, and army coordination.
- Refine tactical AI, combat feedback, and terrain interactions.
- Improve animations and develop additional factions.
